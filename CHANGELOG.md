## 1.0.1 - Initial release

- Port of Thaumic Energistics to Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge) on top of Thaumaturge and Applied Energistics 2.
- ME Essentia Terminal, Import Bus, Export Bus and Storage Bus.
- Essentia storage cells from 1k to 256k, plus a creative cell, with their components and the Diffusion and Coalescence cores.
- Infinity Essentia Cell with practically unlimited storage, infused with the Terminus aspect of Thaumaturge Avaritia.
- ME Arcane Crafting Terminal with a wand slot, six primal crystal slots and an upgrade slot.
- Wireless Arcane Crafting Terminal and Wireless Essentia Terminal, both addable to the AE2WTLib universal terminal.
- Arcane Charging Upgrade: recharges the wand stored in the arcane terminal with vis from the local aura.
- Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal circuits and processors, pressed in the inscriber; the essentia storage components ask for them.
- ExtendedAE support: tiered Assembler Matrix craft, pattern and speed cores, and infinite cells of arcane stone and the six primal vis crystals.
- Advanced AE support: essentia values for its items in the Thaumaturge aspect index.
- Infusion Provider block.
- Essentia can be dragged from JEI into the bus filters.
- Thaumonomicon research category with an entry for every part.
- Terminals and buses follow the cable color like the base AE2 ones.
- English and Spanish translations and full datagen.
