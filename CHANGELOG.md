## 1.0.1 - Compats, processors and new identity

- The mod id is now `thaumaenergistics` and the package `velrondev.thaumaenergistics`. The display name is Thauma Energistics.
- Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal circuits and processors for AE2, pressed in the inscriber; the essentia storage components ask for them.
- AE2 Things support: typeless essentia DISKs from 1k to 256k.
- AE2 MEGA Things and MEGA Cells support: MEGA essentia DISKs and cells from 1M to 256M.
- ExtendedAE support: tiered Assembler Matrix craft, pattern and speed cores, and infinite cells of arcane stone and the six primal vis crystals.
- Advanced AE support: essentia values for its items in the Thaumaturge aspect index.
- Every compat recipe can be switched off in the new `thaumaenergistics-compat.toml` config.
- New logo.

## 1.0.0

- Port of Thaumic Energistics to Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge 21.1.262) on top of Thaumaturge and Applied Energistics 2.
- ME Essentia Terminal, Import Bus, Export Bus and Storage Bus.
- Essentia storage cells from 1k to 256k, plus a creative cell, with their components and the Diffusion and Coalescence cores.
- ME Arcane Crafting Terminal with a wand slot, six primal crystal slots and an upgrade slot.
- Wireless Arcane Crafting Terminal that opens the same GUI, and it can be added to the AE2WTLib universal terminal when that mod is present.
- Arcane Charging Upgrade: recharges the wand stored in the arcane terminal with vis from the local aura.
- Infusion Provider block.
- Essentia can be dragged from JEI into the bus filters.
- Thaumonomicon research category with an entry for every part.
- Remastered textures, English and Spanish translations, and full datagen.
